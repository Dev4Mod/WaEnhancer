[NEW]
* Added support for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business 2.26.46.xx
* Voice note speed slider: long press the speed badge of a voice note to choose any speed
* High quality voice notes, recorded at 48 kHz with a higher bitrate
* Option to choose the noise suppression level of voice notes
* Option to download video notes
* The status viewer now shows how long the status has left before it expires
* WhatsApp's own app themes are turned off while the custom colors are on, and come back when they are turned off

* Fixed "Disable profile photo in status" not loading on newer WhatsApp versions
* Fixed the open chat not being detected in the tablet layout
* Fixed the plugin loading notification getting stuck when WhatsApp was closed while loading

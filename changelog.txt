[NEW]
* Added support for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business 2.26.42.xx and 2.26.43.xx
* You now get a notification when a call is blocked. You can turn it off in the privacy settings

[CHANGED]
* A call block set for a specific contact now takes priority over your general call block settings
* Removed the "Bootloader Spoofer"(obsolete) option because it could make WhatsApp crash during account recovery. If you see the unofficial WhatsApp message, use the "Custom KeyBox" option with your own keybox.xml file

[FIXED]
* Fixed WhatsApp crashing during account recovery
* Fixed the other person sometimes seeing your message as delivered even when you had chosen to hide this
* Fixed hidden "seen" messages not being saved for some contacts, so the blue tick could not be sent to them later
* Fixed the same message being counted more than once when sending the blue tick
* Fixed the blue tick for statuses not being remembered
* Fixed "Remove sticker white outline" only working when "Confirmation Before Sending Sticker" was also turned on
